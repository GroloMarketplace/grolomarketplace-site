Grolo Privacy Policy Last Updated: October 2026 Introduction Grolo ("Grolo", "we", "our", or "us") is a community-driven marketplace that helps users discover local businesses, products, services, and recommendations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information. Information We Collect We may collect information that you provide directly, including: Name and profile information Email address and account credentials Profile photos and content you upload Messages exchanged through the platform Reviews, comments, recommendations, and marketplace listings Customer support communications Information Collected Automatically When you use Grolo, we may automatically collect certain information including: Device information App usage information Crash reports and diagnostics Push notification tokens Approximate location information when provided by the device How We Use Information We use information to: Provide and operate Grolo services Create and manage user accounts Enable messaging and community features Improve platform performance and reliability Personalize marketplace experiences Provide customer support Detect fraud, abuse, or security incidents Comply with legal obligations Sharing Information We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate Grolo, including cloud hosting, analytics, authentication, messaging, and infrastructure providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety, security, or rights of Grolo, our users, or the public. Public Content Profile information, reviews, comments, marketplace listings, recommendations, and other community contributions may be visible to other users of Grolo. Data Security We take reasonable measures to help protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Your Choices You may update your profile information, manage notifications, or contact us regarding your account and privacy preferences. Children's Privacy Grolo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will become effective upon posting the updated policy on our website.
